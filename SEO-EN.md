**My Working Philosophy: Seeing the Whole Person**

I firmly believe that everyone already carries within them the capacity for change, regulation and growth. My work isn't about "fixing" you or handing you ready-made advice — it's about creating the conditions in which lasting development can unfold.

I bring together evidence-based psychology, systemic therapy, body-oriented approaches (Somatic Experiencing®), parts work, and mindfulness into an integrative process that meets you on every level — mental, emotional, physical, relational, and in matters of existential meaning. Rather than thinking in categories, I look at the underlying connections — how life history, the nervous system, relationship patterns and life context all shape our experience.

**01 Understanding ****&**** Contextualising Patterns**

Our present-day patterns of thinking, feeling and behaving are not malfunctions. They are intelligent adaptations our system made to earlier life circumstances, relationships, or difficult situations. In the past, these strategies were vital for survival or protection — today, though, they can get in our way at work, in relationships, or in daily life.

Together, we look at your recurring dynamics without judgment. We gently explore what function these patterns once served and why they persist today. By understanding their origins and triggers, old automatic responses lose their grip — creating room for conscious, new choices.

**02 Involving the Nervous System ****&**** the Body**

Understanding on a purely intellectual level is often not enough to create deep change. The mind may grasp logically that a situation is safe — while the body, our nervous system, continues to respond with stress, tension, freeze, or overwhelm.

Through body-oriented approaches such as Somatic Experiencing®, you learn to notice and understand the subtle signals of your nervous system. We work to gently release stress and restore your organism's capacity for self-regulation. The result is growing inner stability, greater emotional resilience, and a new, tangible sense of presence in everyday life.

**03 Integrating Inner Conflicts ****&**** Parts**

In challenging periods of life, we often experience inner contradictions: one part of us wants change, another seeks safety; one part wants to set clear boundaries, another doesn't want to disappoint anyone. These inner tugs-of-war cost an enormous amount of energy.

Drawing on the Internal Family Systems (IFS) model, we meet your different inner parts with curiosity and appreciation. Rather than pushing away unwanted impulses or feelings, we create a protected space in which all parts can be heard. By clarifying the underlying needs and fears of these parts, inner blocks dissolve — and inner conflict gives way to clarity, self-compassion, and the capacity to act.

**04 Aligning Life With Your Own Compass of Values**

Mindful presence creates the space in which freedom of choice becomes possible. Lasting fulfilment and psychological health arise when our daily actions, decisions, and relationships align with our deepest personal values. Together, we work out what truly matters to you — beyond outside expectations, social pressure, or learned patterns of adaptation. Step by step, we shape a life path that feels coherent, authentic, and fulfilling from within.
