# Theresa Frische — Systemic & Integrative Counsellor

> Online, international counselling practice for individuals and couples
> navigating change, transition, burnout, and life across borders.
> Sessions are offered in English and German.

## About

Theresa Frische is a Systemic & Integrative Counsellor (Systemische &
Integrative Beraterin) offering online counselling worldwide. The practice
supports high-functioning, cross-cultural internationals, expats, and couples
moving through transition, relational strain, identity questions, burnout, and
overwhelm.

This is a counselling (Beratung) practice. Counselling is not psychotherapy,
medical treatment, or a substitute for either. It does not involve diagnosis or
medical care.

Approach: integrative and context-oriented, drawing on systemic counselling,
somatic awareness (Somatic Experiencing), parts work, and mindfulness-based
practice. Qualifications include an M.Sc. in Clinical Psychology, Somatic
Experiencing practitioner training, and advanced systemic training.

## Services

- Individual counselling (online, 60 minutes, English & German)
- Couples counselling (online, 60–120 minutes, English & German)
- Organisations: keynotes, workshops, in-depth trainings, 8-week wellbeing
  programmes, and confidential employee & leadership counselling
- Weekly Wellbeing Community: a weekly online mindfulness and wellbeing group
  (Tuesdays 20:30–21:30, in English)

The single way to begin is a free 15-minute discovery call. No pricing is
published; sessions are self-pay.
