# Content Expansion Brief — Blog + FAQ (Theresa Frische)

Purpose: expand the blog and FAQ into real, keyword-targeted, traffic-drawing content aimed at
Theresa's niche — high-functioning, cross-cultural internationals, expats, and couples navigating
transition, burnout, and relocation. Claude Code writes the full copy from this brief.

## Ground rules (apply to EVERY new post and FAQ answer)

- Compliance firewall (EN + DE) is absolute. Service = counselling only. Never (as a service
  claim): therapy, psychotherapy, treatment, diagnosis, symptoms, disorder, heal/cure, patient,
  clinical. German: never Therapie/Behandlung/Diagnose/Symptome/Störung/Patient as service terms;
  "counselling" = Beratung, never Therapie.
- Avoid clinical condition labels as things she treats: no "depression," "anxiety disorder,"
  "PTSD/trauma treatment." Use everyday language — stress, overwhelm, low mood, exhaustion,
  disconnection, feeling stuck.
- Every blog post ends with the existing psychoeducation disclaimer. Every answer stays warm,
  plain-English, and non-clinical.
- Write EN first, then a faithful, equally-compliant DE translation.
- Quality over speed — write in batches; do not pad. A shorter genuine answer beats filler.

## PART B — FAQ (expand to a structured, categorised set — ~55–60 questions)

Rebuild the FAQ page grouped into the categories below, with a sticky/jump category nav and one
accordion per category. Concise answers (2–5 sentences), keyword-natural, compliant. Keep the
FAQPage JSON-LD. (Note: Google now limits FAQ rich results mostly to gov/health sites, so treat
schema as an AI/LLM-answer + long-tail play, not a guaranteed rich snippet.)

Getting Started
- How do I get started?
- What happens in the free 15-minute discovery call?
- What can I expect in a first session?
- How do I know if counselling is right for me?
- Do I need a specific reason to start counselling?
- How many sessions will I need?
- How often would we meet?

Understanding Counselling (positioning-sensitive — write carefully)
- What is counselling?
- What's the difference between counselling, psychotherapy, and coaching?
- Is counselling the same as therapy? (clarify: no)
- What kinds of things can counselling help with?
- Will you diagnose me? (no — non-clinical)
- Is what we talk about confidential?
- Is counselling a replacement for medical or psychological care? (no — echoes disclaimer)
- Can counselling help even if I'm not in crisis? (yes)

Practical & Logistics
- Are sessions online or in person?
- How does online counselling work?
- What languages do you offer?
- How long is a session?
- Which time zones do you work across?
- How do I book, reschedule, or cancel?
- What's your cancellation policy?
- How do sessions work financially? (self-pay; NO price shown — invite contact)
- Are sessions covered by health insurance / Krankenkasse? (self-pay, no diagnosis needed)
- Do you offer flexible or evening times?

For Individuals
- What challenges do you support individuals with?
- Can counselling help with burnout and overwhelm?
- I'm high-functioning but exhausted — is this for me?
- Can counselling help with a major life transition?
- Can counselling help me with identity and direction?
- Can counselling help me manage stress?

For Couples
- How does couples counselling work?
- Do both partners need to attend?
- What can couples counselling help with?
- How long are couples sessions?
- Can we attend from different countries or time zones?
- Is couples counselling only for relationships in crisis? (no)

International & Expat Life
- Do you work with expats and internationals?
- Can we work together if I live in another country?
- Do you understand the challenges of living between cultures?
- Can you help with relocation stress?
- Do you work with cross-cultural couples?

Approach & Methods
- What is your overall approach?
- Do you follow one method or several?
- What is Somatic Experiencing?
- What is systemic counselling?
- What is parts work?
- How do you use mindfulness?

Weekly Wellbeing Community
- What is the Weekly Wellbeing Community?
- When does it meet?
- How do I join?
- What's the format, and is it free?
- Do I need any experience with mindfulness?

For Organisations
- Do you work with companies?
- What formats do you offer for organisations?
- Can you deliver a one-off workshop or keynote?
- Do you offer employee or leadership support (EAP)?
- Are organisational sessions online or on-site?
- What topics do you cover for teams?
