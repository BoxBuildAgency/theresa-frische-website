### HOME

**A space for what is shifting within you**

For individuals and couples navigating change, transition, and the quieter pressures that often
go unnamed.

Life rarely changes in clear or linear ways. More often, it shifts gradually — until something
begins to feel different within yourself.

Outwardly, you may still be functioning, while inwardly there may be strain, fatigue, or a quiet
sense of disconnection from yourself.

Many people seek support not because something has broken, but because something has shifted —
and familiar ways of coping no longer feel fully sufficient.

*This work is not about fixing what is broken. It is about creating a reflective space to better
understand yourself and move toward a life aligned with your values.*

> *"Theresa creates a space that feels safe, grounded, and deeply supportive. I've become more
> resilient, more present, and more connected to myself."* — Client

**Areas of focus — Who this is for**

This work is for people moving through meaningful transitions — where the inner world asks for
something different than what is already known.

- **Life Transitions** — Relocation, separation, career shifts, loss, parenthood — changes that
  ask for deep emotional adaptation.
- **Relationships** — Communication, emotional distance, recurring conflict, and rebuilding
  understanding and closeness.
- **Identity & Direction** — Periods of uncertainty, reorientation, and questions of purpose and
  life direction.
- **International Life** — The complexity of living across countries, cultures, and shifting
  social environments.
- **Burnout & Overwhelm** — Supporting reconnection and regulation when internal resources feel
  depleted.
- **Personal Growth** — Strengthening clarity, emotional resilience, and a steadier sense of self
  through change.

**My approach — Integrative and context-oriented**

Rather than applying a fixed method, I draw from complementary perspectives to meet each person's
experience with nuance. At the centre of this work is compassionate attention — to what is
happening internally, relationally, and within the wider context of your life.

- **Systemic Counselling** — Exploring relational and contextual patterns, and how they shape
  emotional experience and ways of responding to life.
- **Somatic Awareness** — Attending to the body's responses to stress and change, supporting
  regulation, grounding, and greater internal stability.
- **Parts Work** — Exploring the different parts of yourself that hold conflicting needs, fears, or
  responses — with curiosity and compassion, toward greater integration.
- **Mindfulness-Based Practice** — Developing awareness of thoughts and patterns of reactivity —
  creating space and choice in how we respond.

**Practical information — Working together**

Sessions are offered online, in English and German, to individuals and couples internationally.

- **Format** — Online, worldwide
- **Languages** — English & German
- **Individual sessions** — 60 minutes
- **Couples sessions** — 60–120 minutes
- **Availability** — By appointment

*Ready to take the first step? Book a free 15-minute discovery call — a relaxed, no-obligation way
to begin.*

I know what it feels like when life becomes too much — when stress builds up, relationships feel

Over time, I've come to understand how deeply our nervous system, relationships, and life

### FAQ

**How do I get started?**
The first step is a free 15-minute discovery call. It's a relaxed, no-obligation conversation where
you can share what brings you here, ask any questions, and get a sense of whether working together
feels right.

**What languages do you offer sessions in?**
Sessions are available in both English and German, for individuals and couples internationally.

**Are sessions online or in person?**
Individual and couples sessions are offered online via video, allowing me to work with clients
internationally. For organisations, workshops and trainings can be delivered online or on-site in
Germany and Switzerland.

**How long are sessions, and how often do we meet?**
Individual sessions last 60 minutes; couples sessions run 60–120 minutes. How often we meet is
something we agree on together, based on what feels right for your situation.

**What is Somatic Experiencing, and how is it used in our sessions?**
Somatic Experiencing is a body-oriented approach to working with stress and overwhelming experiences.
It supports the nervous system in releasing held tension and returning to a greater sense of safety
and regulation. It's drawn on alongside systemic and mindfulness-based approaches, depending on what
feels useful for you.

**Is this covered by health insurance (Krankenkasse)?**
Sessions are self-pay and not reimbursed by statutory or private health insurance. This allows for a
flexible, confidential way of working — with no insurance paperwork, no reporting requirements, and no
need to fit your experience into clinical categories.

**How is couples counselling different from individual counselling?**
Couples counselling focuses on relational patterns — communication, connection, and recurring
conflict. Sessions are usually longer (60–120 minutes) to allow space for both perspectives to be
heard.

**How can I join the Weekly Wellbeing Community?**
The community meets online via Teams on Tuesdays from 20:30–21:30, in English. Get in touch using the
contact form to receive more information and a link to join.

**Can my organisation book a one-off session, or only longer programmes?**
Both. I offer everything from single keynote talks and workshops (2–4 hours) to multi-day trainings
and structured 8-week programmes — tailored to what your organisation needs.
